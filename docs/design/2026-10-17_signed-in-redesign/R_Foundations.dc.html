Foundations

One naming rule, one way to set numbers, and one placeholder coin. Every screen in this redesign uses all three.

1 · Naming rule

EyebrowWe Are Strong. We Are Together TitleDonetsk Oblast SubtitleUkraine · 10 Hryvnias · 2025
  1. Title = the specific design. Use the part of variety after the series prefix (“Series: Design”). Otherwise use name, unless it is only country + denomination. Otherwise use the denomination.
  2. Subtitle = country · denomination · year with the mint mark in collector notation (1909-S). Leave out any part the title already says.
  3. Eyebrow = the series name, only when the coin belongs to one. When every coin on a page shares it, show it once in the page header.
  4. The year is never in the title, except for date-style key names such as “1909-S VDB”.

2 · Numerals

Before · Cormorant default (old-style) 11 of 12 · 2025 · 10

The 1 reads as a small-caps “I”, so “11” looks like “II” and the placeholder “10” looks like “IO”.

After · lining in headings, Plex Mono for data 11 of 12 · 2025 · 10 11 of 12 · 92% · 2025

Set font-variant-numeric: lining-nums globally. Counts, years, percentages and specs use IBM Plex Mono with tabular figures. Year group headers move from Cormorant to Plex Mono 500.

3 · Placeholder coin

An intentional, struck-coin disc: a metal tone taken from material, a raised rim, an inner bead ring, the denomination numeral in Plex Sans 600 (always lining) and the unit in tracked caps. A Wikimedia photo will later replace the disc in the same circle, so layouts don’t shift.
